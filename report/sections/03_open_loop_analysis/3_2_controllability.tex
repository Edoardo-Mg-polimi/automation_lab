\subsection{Controllability}

The controllability matrix is
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{C} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        B & A B & A^2B
    \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}

The linearized system is controllable if
\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{rank}(\mathcal{C}) = n,
\end{equation}
where $n$ is the number of states.

For the extended model, $n=3$. Substituting the structure of $A$ and $B$ gives
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{C} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        0 & 0 & k_2\omega_i \\
        0 & k_2\omega_i & -k_2\omega_i^2 \\
        \omega_i & -\omega_i^2 & \omega_i^3
    \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}

Its determinant is
\begin{equation}
    \det(\mathcal{C}) = -k_2^2\omega_i^3.
\end{equation}
Since $k_2 \neq 0$ and $\omega_i \neq 0$, the determinant is non-zero. Therefore the model is fully
controllable.

The numerical check gives
\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{rank}(\mathcal{C}) = 3.
\end{equation}

\begin{lstlisting}[style=matlabstyle,caption={Controllability check.}]
Mr = ctrb(A_ext, B_ext);
rank_Mr = rank(Mr);
disp(rank_Mr);
\end{lstlisting}
