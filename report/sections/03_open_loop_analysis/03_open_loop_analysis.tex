\section{Open-loop analysis}

The open-loop analysis is performed on the linearized model around the selected equilibrium point
$x_e = 7\ \mathrm{mm}$ and $i_e = 0.707\ \mathrm{A}$. The purpose is to characterize the plant before
designing the position controllers. In particular, the analysis verifies whether the model is unstable,
controllable and observable, and it identifies the operating limits that must be respected during
simulation and experimental tests.

The model used in this section is the extended plant introduced in the previous chapter, where the
mechanical dynamics are combined with the closed current loop:
\begin{equation}
    \vect{x}_{ext} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        \Delta x & \Delta \dot{x} & \Delta i
    \end{bmatrix}^T,
    \qquad
    u_{ext} = \Delta i_{ref}.
\end{equation}

Using $k_1 = 2802.86$, $k_2 = 27.73$ and $\omega_i = 529.42\ \mathrm{rad/s}$, the numerical
state-space realization is
\begin{equation}
    A_{ext} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        0 & 1 & 0 \\
        2802.86 & 0 & 27.73 \\
        0 & 0 & -529.42
    \end{bmatrix},
    \qquad
    B_{ext} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        0 \\
        0 \\
        529.42
    \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}

The measured outputs are the ball position variation and the current variation:
\begin{equation}
    C_{ext} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        1 & 0 & 0 \\
        0 & 0 & 1
    \end{bmatrix},
    \qquad
    D_{ext} = 0.
\end{equation}
