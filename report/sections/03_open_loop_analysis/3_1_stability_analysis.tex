\subsection{Stability analysis}

The open-loop poles are obtained from the eigenvalues of the state matrix $A$:
\begin{equation}
    \lambda_i = \mathrm{eig}(A).
\end{equation}

For the extended model, the eigenvalues are
\begin{equation}
    \lambda(A) =
    \left\{
        +52.94,\;
        -52.94,\;
        -22.67
    \right\}\ \mathrm{rad/s}.
    \label{eq:open_loop_poles}
\end{equation}


The positive pole is a direct consequence of the
open-loop magnetic levitation dynamics: if the ball moves away from the equilibrium point, the
magnetic force variation does not naturally restore the position.
\begin{equation}
    \max_i \Re\{\lambda_i\} = 52.94 > 0.
\end{equation}
Therefore the equilibrium is unstable without feedback control.

poles_ol = eig(A);
disp(poles_ol);
\end{lstlisting}
