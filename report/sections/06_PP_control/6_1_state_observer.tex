\textcolor{red}{EXPLAIN WHY WE NEED AN OBSERVER}
A Luenberger observer is used to estimate $\Delta x$ and $\Delta \dot{x}$ from the position measurement. In \texttt{SS\_model\_PP.m} the observer poles are placed at
\begin{equation}
    p_{obs} = \{-500, -600\}.
\end{equation}
The observer gain is computed with
\begin{equation}
    L_{obs} = \mathrm{place}(A_x^T, C_x^T, p_{obs})^T.
\end{equation}

\textcolor{red}{ACKERMANN FORMULA}

The observer dynamics are
\begin{equation}
    \dot{\hat{\vect{x}}} = (A_x - L_{obs} C_x)\hat{\vect{x}} + B_x \Delta i_{ref} + L_{obs} \Delta x.
\end{equation}
