\subsection{Pole placement controller}

The controller uses full-state feedback on the extended model. The desired closed-loop poles in \texttt{SS\_model\_PP.m} are
\begin{equation}
    p_{ctrl} = \{-70, -75, -\omega_i\},
\end{equation}
and the feedback gain is computed with
\begin{equation}
    K_{pp} = \mathrm{place}(A_{ext}, B_{ext}, p_{ctrl}).
\end{equation}

The control law is
\begin{equation}
    \Delta i_{ref} = -K_{pp} \hat{\vect{x}}_{ext} + K_{ref} \Delta x_{ref}.
\end{equation}
The reference scaling used in \texttt{SS\_model\_PP.m} is
\begin{equation}
    K_{ref} = -\frac{1}{C_{pos}\left(A_{ext} - B_{ext} K_{pp}\right)^{-1} B_{ext}},
\end{equation}
with $C_{pos} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$.
