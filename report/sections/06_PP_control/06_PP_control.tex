\subsection{State-space model for Pole Placement}

The Pole Placement controller is designed on the extended linearized model obtained by combining the mechanical subsystem with the closed-loop current dynamics. The state vector is defined as
\[
x_{ext} =
\begin{bmatrix}
\Delta x & \Delta \dot{x} & \Delta i
\end{bmatrix}^T ,
\]
where \(\Delta x\) is the ball position variation around the equilibrium point, \(\Delta \dot{x}\) is the velocity variation and \(\Delta i\) is the coil current variation.

The control input is the current reference variation:
\[
u_{ext} = \Delta i_{ref}.
\]

The extended model is:
\[
\dot{x}_{ext} = A_{ext}x_{ext} + B_{ext}u_{ext},
\]
with
\[
A_{ext} =
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 1 & 0\\
k_1 & 0 & k_2\\
0 & 0 & -\omega_i
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
B_{ext} =
\begin{bmatrix}
0\\
0\\
\omega_i
\end{bmatrix}.
\]

The output matrix is chosen in order to observe both the ball position and the coil current:
\[
C_{ext} =
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0\\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}.
\]
