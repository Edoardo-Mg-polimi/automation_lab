\subsection{Pole placement controller}

\paragraph{Requirements}
\textcolor{red}{how work pp, why we use $\omega_i$}

\paragraph{Simple pole placement}
The controller uses full-state feedback on the entire system model. The desired closed-loop poles in \texttt{SS\_model\_PP.m} are
\begin{equation}
    p_{ctrl} = \{-70, -75, -\omega_i\},
\end{equation}
and the feedback gain is computed with
\begin{equation}
    K_{pp} = \mathrm{place}(A_{ext}, B_{ext}, p_{ctrl}).
\end{equation}

The control law is
\begin{equation}
    \Delta i_{ref} = -K_{pp} \hat{\vect{x}}_{ext} + K_{ref} \Delta x_{ref}.
\end{equation}
The reference scaling used in \texttt{SS\_model\_PP.m} is
\begin{equation}
    K_{ref} = -\frac{1}{C_{pos}\left(A_{ext} - B_{ext} K_{pp}\right)^{-1} B_{ext}},
\end{equation}
with $C_{pos} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$.


\paragraph{Pole placement with integral action}
To remove steady-state error and achieve the position reference, an integrator on the position error is added:
\begin{equation}
    \dot{x_i} = \Delta x_{ref} - \Delta x.
\end{equation}
The augmented state is $\vect{x}_{aug} = \begin{bmatrix} \vect{x}_{ext} & x_i \end{bmatrix}^T$, with
\begin{equation}
    A_{aug} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        A_{ext} & 0 \\
        -C_{pos} & 0
    \end{bmatrix},
    \quad
    B_{aug} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        B_{ext} \\
        0
    \end{bmatrix},
    \quad
    B_{r} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        0 \\
        1
    \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}

\textcolor{red}{why the integrator pole is at...}
In \texttt{SS\_model\_PP.m} the integral pole is chosen as $p_{int} = -3$ and the augmented pole set is
\begin{equation}
    p_{ctrl,aug} = \{-70, -75, p_{int}, -\omega_i\}.
\end{equation}
The gain is obtained with
\begin{equation}
    K_{aug} = \mathrm{place}(A_{aug}, B_{aug}, p_{ctrl,aug}) = \begin{bmatrix} K_x & K_i \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
