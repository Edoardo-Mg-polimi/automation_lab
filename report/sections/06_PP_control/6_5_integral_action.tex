\subsection{Pole placement with integral action}

To remove steady-state error, an integrator on the position error is added:
\begin{equation}
    \dot{\xi} = \Delta x_{ref} - \Delta x.
\end{equation}
The augmented state is $\vect{x}_{aug} = \begin{bmatrix} \vect{x}_{ext} & \xi \end{bmatrix}^T$, with
\begin{equation}
    A_{aug} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        A_{ext} & 0 \\
        -C_{pos} & 0
    \end{bmatrix},
    \quad
    B_{aug} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        B_{ext} \\
        0
    \end{bmatrix},
    \quad
    B_{r} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        0 \\
        1
    \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}

In \texttt{SS\_model\_PP.m} the integral pole is chosen as $p_{int} = -3$ and the augmented pole set is
\begin{equation}
    p_{ctrl,aug} = \{-70, -75, p_{int}, -\omega_i\}.
\end{equation}
The gain is obtained with
\begin{equation}
    K_{aug} = \mathrm{place}(A_{aug}, B_{aug}, p_{ctrl,aug}) = \begin{bmatrix} K_x & K_i \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
