\subsubsection{Stability margins}

For the LQI design, the open-loop system used to compute margins is
\begin{equation}
    L_{lqi}(s) = K_{aug}\left(sI - A_{aug}\right)^{-1} B_{aug}.
\end{equation}
The gain margin, phase margin, and crossover frequencies are reported here based on the values returned by 
texttt{margin} in \texttt{SS\_model\_LQ.m}.
