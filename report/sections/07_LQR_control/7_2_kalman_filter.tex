\subsection{State estimation with Kalman filter}

State estimation uses a continuous-time Kalman filter (LQE) on the mechanical model. The measured output is the position, with
\begin{equation}
    C_{meas} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Process noise is applied on the acceleration channel,
\begin{equation}
    G_{kf} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix},
\end{equation}
and the tuning values in \texttt{SS\_model\_LQ.m} are
\begin{equation}
    \sigma_a = 20, \qquad \sigma_{yx} = 0.1\times10^{-3}.
\end{equation}

The covariance matrices are
\begin{equation}
    Q_{kf} = \sigma_a^2, \qquad R_{kf} = \sigma_{yx}^2,
\end{equation}
and the Kalman gain is
\begin{equation}
    L_{kf} = \mathrm{lqe}(A_x, G_{kf}, C_{meas}, Q_{kf}, R_{kf}).
\end{equation}
