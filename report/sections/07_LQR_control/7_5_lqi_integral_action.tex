\subsection{LQI with integral action}

To improve steady-state accuracy, an integrator on the position error is added:
\begin{equation}
    \dot{\xi} = \Delta x_{ref} - \Delta x.
\end{equation}
The augmented state is $\vect{x}_{aug} = \begin{bmatrix} \vect{x}_{ext} & \xi \end{bmatrix}^T$, with
\begin{equation}
    A_{aug} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        A_{ext} & 0 \\
        -C_{pos} & 0
    \end{bmatrix},
    \qquad
    B_{aug} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        B_{ext} \\
        0
    \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
The LQI weights in \texttt{SS\_model\_LQ.m} are
\begin{equation}
    Q_{aug} = \mathrm{diag}(5000, 5\times10^{-1}, 10^{-9}, 9\times10^{4}),
    \qquad
    R_{aug} = 5\times10^{-4}.
\end{equation}
The resulting gain is
\begin{equation}
    K_{aug} = \mathrm{lqr}(A_{aug}, B_{aug}, Q_{aug}, R_{aug}) = \begin{bmatrix} K_{x,lqi} & K_{i,lqi} \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
