\subsection{LQR with integral action}

The controller is designed directly on the augmented model with integral action. All variables are
deviations from the equilibrium point, and the input is $\Delta i_{ref}$.

The integral state is defined as
\begin{equation}
    \dot{x_i} = x_{ref} - x.
\end{equation}
The augmented state is
\begin{equation}
    \vect{x}_{aug} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
         x &  \dot{x} & i & x_i
    \end{bmatrix}^T.
\end{equation}
The augmented matrices are
\begin{equation}
    A_{aug} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        A & 0 \\
        -C_{pos} & 0
    \end{bmatrix},
    \qquad
    B_{aug} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        B \\
        0
    \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}

With 
\begin{equation}
    C_{\mathrm{pos}}
    =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        1 & 0 & 0
    \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}


The quadratic cost minimized by the controller is
\begin{equation}
    J
    =
    \int_0^{\infty}
    \left(
        \vect{x}_{aug}^T Q_{aug}\vect{x}_{aug}
        +
        \Delta i_{ref}^T R_{aug}\Delta i_{ref}
    \right)
    \dd t,
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
    Q_{aug}
    =
    \mathrm{diag}
    \left(
        q_x,\;
        q_{\dot{x}},\;
        q_i,\;
        q_{x_i}
    \right)
    \qquad
    R_{aug}=r
\end{equation}
The terms of $Q_{aug}$ penalize position, velocity, current variation and accumulated position error.
$R_{aug}$ penalizes the requested current variation.

The final best experimental tuning is
\begin{equation}
    Q_{aug,exp}
    =
    \mathrm{diag}
    \left(
        5000,\;
        5,\;
        10^{-9},\;
        3\times10^{-4}
    \right),
    \qquad
    R_{aug,exp}=0.05.
\end{equation}
The simulation parameters work with the ideal model but are too aggressive on the real setup. The
experimental parameters account for noise, nonlinearities and current saturation, but are not optimal in
nominal simulation.

For a chosen pair $(Q_{aug},R_{aug})$, the feedback gain is
\begin{equation}
    K_{aug}
    =
    \mathrm{lqr}(A_{aug}, B_{aug}, Q_{aug}, R_{aug})
    =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        K_{x,lqi} & K_{i,lqi}
    \end{bmatrix}.
\end{equation}
The control law is therefore
\begin{equation}
    \Delta i_{ref}
    =
    -K_{x,lqi}
    \begin{bmatrix}
         x &  \dot{x} &  i
    \end{bmatrix}^T
    -K_{i,lqi}x_i.
\end{equation}
