\subsubsection{Time-Domain Validation}
The time-domain validation of the current controller was performed by applying a step reference equal to the equilibrium current. The results are shown in Figure Y. 
The response exhibits a fast rise time but also periodic oscillations in steady state, attributed to mechanical disturbances present in the system. This behavior did not compromise closed-loop performance: during position controller validation, the current loop successfully tracked the time-varying reference generated by the outer loop, as shown in Figure H. 
A potential improvement would be to reduce the current loop bandwidth and redesign the position controller accordingly, which may reduce sensitivity to the mechanical disturbance at the cost of slower current tracking.
