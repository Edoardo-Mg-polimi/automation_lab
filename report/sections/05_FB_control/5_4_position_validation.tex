\subsubsection{Time-Domain Validation}
The time-domain validation of the position controller was performed with a constant reference equal to
the equilibrium position. A slope limiter was added so that the reference reached its final value in
$4~\mathrm{s}$, reducing the initial transient demand.

\paragraph{Neighborhood of linearized point}
Additional tests were performed by changing the constant reference value around the equilibrium point
to assess the practical range of validity of the linearized model. The decreasing-reference test shows
slightly worse tracking, likely due to consecutive hardware use and changing thermal conditions.

\subsubsection{Frequency-Domain Validation}
The frequency-domain validation was performed by applying sinusoidal references around the equilibrium
position at $1$, $6$ and $20~\mathrm{rad/s}$.

Before extracting the frequency response, the measured signals were filtered with a zero-phase
\textit{filtfilt} routine to reduce high-frequency noise.
The cutoff frequency was selected for each test case, ensuring the fundamental sinusoidal component was preserved while suppressing noise.
