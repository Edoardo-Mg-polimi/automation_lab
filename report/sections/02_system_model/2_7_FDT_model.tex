\subsection{Mechanical linearized model}

Starting from the complete linearized state-space model, the mechanical subsystem can be isolated by considering only the position and velocity states, and by taking the current variation $\Delta i$ as input.

\subsubsection{Mechanical state space model}

The mechanical state vector is defined as

\begin{equation}
    x_m =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        \Delta x \\
        \Delta \dot{x}
    \end{bmatrix},
\end{equation}

with input $\Delta i$ and output $\Delta x$. The mechanical linearized model is therefore

\begin{equation}
    \dot{x}_m(t) = A_x x_m(t) + B_x \Delta i(t),
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
    y(t) = C_x x_m(t) + D_x \Delta i(t),
\end{equation}

where

\begin{equation}
    A_x =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        0 & 1 \\
        k_1 & 0
    \end{bmatrix},
    \qquad
    B_x =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        0 \\
        k_2
    \end{bmatrix},
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
    C_x =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        1 & 0
    \end{bmatrix},
    \qquad
    D_x = 0.
\end{equation}

The coefficients are

\begin{equation}
    k_1 =
    \frac{2K_m i_e^2}
    {m\left(l - 2r - x_e\right)^3},
    \qquad
    k_2 =
    \frac{2K_m i_e}
    {m\left(l - 2r - x_e\right)^2}.
\end{equation}

\subsubsection{FDT mechanical model}

Starting from the state-space linearized model of the mechanical subsystem

\begin{align}
    \dot{x}_m(t) &= A_x x_m(t) + B_x \Delta i(t), \\
    y(t) &= C_x x_m(t) + D_x \Delta i(t),
\end{align}

the corresponding transfer function is obtained as

\begin{equation}
    G_x(s) =
    \frac{\Delta X(s)}{\Delta I(s)}
    =
    C_x(sI - A_x)^{-1}B_x + D_x.
\end{equation}

Substituting the mechanical matrices gives

\begin{equation}
    G_x(s) =
    \frac{k_2}{s^2 - k_1}.
\end{equation}

The poles of the mechanical subsystem are

\[
    p_{1,2} = \pm \sqrt{k_1} = \pm 52.9420
\]

Since one pole is positive, the mechanical subsystem is open-loop unstable.
